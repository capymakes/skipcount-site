What the app does

SkipCount counts jump rope skips using the camera and on-device motion analysis, and keeps a history of your sessions. Counting depends on lighting, distance from the camera and how you move, and it may occasionally miss or over-count a jump. The numbers are for your own motivation and record; they are not medical or professional fitness measurements.

Your health
