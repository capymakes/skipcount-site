Every jump, counted.

Prop up your phone, step back and jump. SkipCount watches through the camera and keeps the score, out loud, so you never have to look down or lose count again.

7-day free trial. No account needed.

How it works

  1. Prop it upLean your iPhone or iPad against something at about knee height, camera facing you.
  2. Step backTwo or three steps, until your whole body is in the frame.
  3. JumpAfter a short countdown, every landing is counted. The number is spoken aloud every 50 jumps, and your sets close themselves when you stop to rest.

What you get

Live count and tempo

Jumps, jumps per minute, the current set and the session total, big enough to read from across the room.

A daily goal and a streak

Pick a number that fits your week. The ring fills as you go, and the streak counts the days you showed up.

Challenges sized to you

Daily, weekly and monthly targets built from your own goal and how often you actually train.

30 badges and 10 levels

Bronze, silver and gold. The first one lands on your very first workout.

History and calendar

Every session kept, a two-week chart, and a month calendar that shows every day you jumped.

Three ways to train

Just jump, a timed sprint, or stop when today's goal is done.

Your camera stays yours

The video is analysed live on your device to find your body and count the landings. Nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Delete the app and everything it knew is gone.
